Privacy Policy

Your privacy and confidentiality are fundamental to our legal practice and client relationships.

Last Updated: August 13, 2025

Masinde Wanyonyi & Company Advocates ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage our legal services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number, mailing address)
  • Professional information (company name, job title, business details)
  • Legal matter details (case information, documents, correspondence)
  • Financial information (for billing and payment processing)
  • Identification documents (as required for legal verification)

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and location information
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Referral source and exit pages

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Providing legal services and representation
  • Communicating with clients about their legal matters
  • Processing payments and managing billing
  • Maintaining case files and legal records
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Improving our website and services
  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer support
  • Sending updates about legal developments (with consent)

3. Attorney-Client Privilege

We strictly adhere to attorney-client privilege laws and professional ethics rules. Information shared with us in the context of legal representation is protected by:

  • Attorney-client privilege under Kenyan law
  • Professional confidentiality obligations
  • Legal professional privilege
  • Duty of confidentiality as defined by the Law Society of Kenya

This privileged information will not be disclosed to third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law or court order.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

With Your Consent

  • When you explicitly authorize us to share information
  • For specific purposes you have approved

Legal Requirements

  • To comply with court orders or legal processes
  • To meet regulatory reporting requirements
  • To prevent fraud or protect legal rights

Service Providers

  • Trusted third-party providers who assist in delivering our services
  • Technology providers (subject to confidentiality agreements)
  • Professional service providers (accountants, IT support, etc.)

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

  • Secure physical access controls to our offices
  • Encrypted digital communications and file storage
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Employee training on confidentiality and data protection
  • Secure disposal of documents and electronic media
  • Multi-factor authentication for sensitive systems

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide ongoing legal services
  • Meet professional obligations and ethics requirements
  • Comply with legal and regulatory retention periods
  • Handle potential future legal matters or appeals

Generally, client files are maintained for a minimum of seven (7) years after the conclusion of legal matters, or as required by applicable law.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information we hold
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability: Request transfer of your information to another service provider
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your information
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

8. Website-Specific Information

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These may include:

  • Essential cookies for website functionality
  • Analytics cookies to understand website usage
  • Preference cookies to remember your settings

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. International Data Transfers

In certain circumstances, your information may be transferred outside of Kenya for processing or storage. When this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information, including:

  • Adequate protection measures as required by Kenyan data protection laws
  • Contractual protections with service providers
  • Compliance with international data transfer regulations

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy on our website
  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Notify existing clients of material changes
  • Obtain consent where required by law
