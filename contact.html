Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and how we can help you.

How much do you charge for consultations?

Our consultation fees vary depending on the complexity of your legal matter. We offer an initial consultation to discuss your case and provide a fee estimate. Contact us for specific pricing information.

What documents should I bring to my first meeting?

Please bring any relevant documents related to your legal matter, including contracts, correspondence, court papers, identification documents, and any other materials that may help us understand your situation.

Do you handle cases outside of Nairobi?

Yes, we handle cases throughout Kenya. While our main office is in Nairobi, we represent clients across the country and can travel for important meetings and court appearances.

How long does it take to resolve a legal matter?

The timeline varies greatly depending on the type and complexity of your case. During your consultation, we'll provide a realistic estimate of the time frame based on our experience with similar matters.

Can I schedule weekend or evening appointments?

While our regular hours are Monday through Friday, we understand that legal matters can be urgent. We offer flexible scheduling, including weekend and evening appointments when necessary.
